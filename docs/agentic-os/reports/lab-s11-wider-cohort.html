Widening the cohort to 30 cases per stratum: the DB fixes hold, the funnel readings hold, and K=8 buys throughput not latency

One paid session ran the difficulty-stratified benchmark at three times the earlier sample (90 cases per lane, 360 per pass) on bundle v40, the first bundle with the Bolt timeout, lookup indexes and static anchor guard live. Two passes over the same cases, differing only in client concurrency (K=4 and K=8), on a TP=2 Qwen3.6-27B monolith with reasoning off.

Terminology. The agents measured here run an iterative Text2Cypher / Text2SPARQL loop in the GraphCoT style: each turn thinks, calls inspect_graph_schema or a whole-query tool (execute_cypher / execute_sparql), observes the result and continues, up to six turns. They are not the original Graph-CoT action set (RetrieveNode / NodeFeature / NeighbourCheck / NodeDegree, hop by hop); that lane is being added as lab 017 for a direct comparison.

2 · The three DB fixes (lab 012, items 1–3), measured live

Lookup indexes at start ONLINE

All ten GRBench indexes (id, name, title, TEXT on name/title, source id, type+workspace, relation name, two composite) reported ONLINE in the dozer_indexes phase, which now runs after DozerDB readiness. lab-s9 died here a day earlier because the step ran before the database accepted Bolt.

Transaction timeout 15 s fires

723 execute_cypher calls in the two GRBench single passes: p50 16 ms, p90 35 ms, three at the bound (15.6–16.5 s), each returned as rejected and the agent moved on. In lab-s3/s5/s8 single label scans ran 43–63 s and dominated the lane.

Static anchor guard repairs validate, rarely retrieve

unanchored_scan rejected 16 (k4) and 20 (k8) first attempts across 9 and 12 cases. In every case the agent produced a validated query afterwards; 1 of 9 and 3 of 12 of those cases reached non-empty rows. The guard converts a 60-second scan into an instant rejection, but the repair mostly lands on another dead end.

Where the wall goes now

GRBench single, k4: LLM share 0.95–0.995 per stratum, graph-DB share 0.005–0.05, framework residual 0. The database is no longer measurable in this lane's wall; what remains is the six-turn reasoning loop.

What N = 30 confirms from the N = 10 readings

  • Difficulty acts on finalisation, not retrieval (GRBench single). Retrieval triggers 30/30 and validates 29–30 in every stratum; non-empty rows 24–30. Answers: 9 easy, 16 medium, 2 hard. Hard exhausts the six-turn budget in 28 of 30 cases while holding rows.
  • Topology dominates (GRBench multi). 3–4 of 30 validated per stratum, 0 non-empty. missing_workspace_scope was raised 306 times in 90 cases (3.4 per case): the specialist never learns the scope template from the rejection. This is the target of 012 item 4.
  • The easy-stratum retrieval skip is real (GTSQA multi). 12 of 30 easy cases trigger retrieval, against 29 and 30 for medium and hard; wall 3.5 s vs 67–72 s. The manager answers easy questions from memory. 012 item 5.
  • GTSQA single is a reasoning loop. Triggers 24–29, validates 23–28, non-empty 11–16, answered 0 in every stratum, exhausted 22–29. LLM share 0.997, 18–20 k reasoning tokens per case. No DB lever applies here.
  • Serving shape leaves behaviour alone. K=8 changes every funnel column by at most three cases per cell; it changes wall time and energy only.

5 · Observability

The in-instance OTLP receiver captured 14 996 server-side spans. vLLM emitted a chat and an llm_request span for every request; 2 085 of 2 099 client requests in the K=4 pass (99.3 %) matched a server span through the W3C traceparent the runner sends, so the per-request stage table (queue, TTFT, prefill, decode, client-minus-server) exists for both passes.

Defect found and fixed: every runner span carried service.name=vllm. The appliance entrypoint exports OTEL_SERVICE_NAME=vllm for the engine, and the pass script's exporter inherited it. The exporter now reads its own variable (SEOCHO_OTEL_SERVICE_NAME, default seocho-agentic-os) and a shell test asserts it; the fix is unvalidated live until the next pass.

6 · What this changes in the plan

  • Serving default stands: one TP=2 engine per two-GPU box; K=4 when latency per case matters, K=8 when a batch of cases should finish sooner and cheaper (−40 % energy).
  • 012 items 1–3 are validated; close them when the lane lands on main. The DB is out of the wall; nothing further on the DozerDB side is justified by this data.
  • 012 item 4 (productive repair loop) is the next lever with evidence: 306 missing_workspace_scope rejections in 90 GRBench-multi cases, and anchor-guard repairs that validate without retrieving. A scope template injected on the first rejection and a stop on repeated identical queries are the two concrete changes.
  • 012 item 5 (retrieval trigger on easy questions) has its N=30 number: 12 of 30. A manager-side classification that forces the tool for schema-answerable questions can be measured against this cell.
  • GRBench single finalisation (hard: 2 answered, 28 exhausted while holding rows) is a prompt/turn-budget question, not a retrieval one; it belongs with the finalisation rule proposed after lab-s3.
